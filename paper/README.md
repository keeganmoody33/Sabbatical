## Freeze in one sentence

A freeze is a locked snapshot of evidence. After it, those artifacts are not recoded. Later work may add a source or a sidecar overlay. It may not rewrite the earlier extracts.

## Rules that survive into published copy

- No dashes as punctuation.
- Never print 247, 11 interviews, 4.45 percent, 321, or 325 as this freeze's finding.
- Unit is 298 applications, not 298 companies.
- Two registers. Do not union opportunity interviews into 14/298.
- Completeness is unmeasured. Do not print a percent.
- Naming pass before Mixmax, Mercor, Mobb, Kivira.health, and BCOFA in public copy. This working record still names them with that flag.
- Dual role (subject equals author) stays in Methods and Acknowledgments.

## Claims blocks

Section files that state quantities end with an HTML `<!-- claims ... -->` table. Tier is `measured`, `estimated`, `memory`, or `unknown`. That is the verification list, not a second census.
