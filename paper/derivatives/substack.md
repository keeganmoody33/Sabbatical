## Suggested hed

## Body

People do not know how many jobs they applied to. The evidence is scattered across ATS mail, applied lists, agents, and DMs, and every source double-counts the others.

I reconstructed my own search, 2025-06-01 through 2026-08-29, as a forensic census. I am the subject and the author. That is a limitation, not a vibe.

A job search has two pipelines. The application census is roles I submitted. The opportunity register is recruiter-initiated processes, referrals, matching-platform contracts, and consulting. Mixing them makes an application-to-interview rate uninterpretable.

The freeze: lock a snapshot, do not recode it later. Gmail and Calendar independently coded to 221 applications. LinkedIn pages 1 to 10 and Jobright added 77. Remaining personal mail, calendar, and a care-package sidecar added zero applications. The census is **298 applications** at 273 companies.

Interviewed is derived from events, never stored. **14** of those 298 reached an interview-set event. Rate **14/298**. All 14 are in the first freeze. The 77 later platform rows carry no interview-set events. That is not a claim that LinkedIn produced no conversations.

Coded offers from the 298: **0**. Offer-language search is closed on one study mailbox and still open on the personal mailbox. Completeness is unmeasured. The Easy Apply versus external ATS overlap was never labeled. I do not print a completeness percentage.

The largest paid outcomes arrived through employment, consulting, or opportunity channels this applications instrument cannot treat as 14/298. Six marketplace evaluator receipts for one of those employers sit in the 298 and did not convert.

If you only look at 14/298, the search looks like a failure. That is what you get when you measure applying and call it the whole search.

Replication needs the same window, the same two registers, and the same freeze discipline. Later sources add. They do not rewrite earlier extracts.
