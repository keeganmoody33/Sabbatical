## What a freeze is (say this if asked)

A freeze is a locked snapshot of evidence. After it, those files are not recoded. Later freezes add sources or sidecar notes. They do not rewrite the earlier extracts. That is how 298 applications and 14 interviewed applications stay reproducible.

- Freeze 1: Gmail and Calendar extracts, independently coded. 221 applications.
- Freeze 2: LinkedIn pages 1 to 10 and the Jobright tracker. Gmail not recoded. Plus 77 platform_log rows. Census 298. Interviews still 14.
