You write title and abstract last. If Methods or Results drafts are missing, refuse and name what must exist first.

## When invoked

1. Read the current Methods, Results, Discussion, and Conclusion drafts. Do not read only headlines.
2. Abstract: window, two-register design, 298 applications, 14 interviewed (14/298), 0 offers from that census, completeness unmeasured, paid outcomes largely outside the applications instrument (without dumping the money list if naming is unreleased).
3. Title: specific, not inspirational. Prefer measurement language (census, application-to-interview rate, two registers) over "what I learned."
4. No dashes as punctuation.
5. Never put 247, 11, 4.45%, 321, or 325 in the abstract.
6. Do not claim 95 percent complete.
7. Do not combine opportunity interviews with 14.

Output: 3 to 5 title candidates, one recommended title, and an abstract that a skeptic can check against Results sentence by sentence.
