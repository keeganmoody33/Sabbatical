## The hole in the method, stated plainly

**Dagster Labs: zero calendar presence.** A phone interview held 2026-03-30, evidenced by six email artifacts and confirmed independently by the subject. Arranged by email, held by phone, never invited to calendar.

**The Kiln: zero calendar presence.** Two founder calls on 2026-03-03 and 03-04, both inside block 4, both arranged by Google Meet links pasted into LinkedIn DMs. This **answers the open question in logs 036 and 037** — nothing followed off-platform, because nothing was ever on the calendar.

**Consequence.** Calendar is a near-complete roster of *invited* meetings. It is blind to meetings arranged by phone, by DM, or by a link pasted into an email body. Combined with the confirmed limit that a personal calendar records invitations rather than attendance, the correct formulation is:

> **Calendar enumerates invited meetings. Email enumerates arranged-but-uninvited meetings and adjudicates which were held. The event layer is the union, and neither source bounds it alone.**

This refines rather than overturns the calendar-first instruction: calendar should have been swept first and completely, and it would have prevented the Orchestry and Pearl errors. It would not have prevented the Dagster error.
