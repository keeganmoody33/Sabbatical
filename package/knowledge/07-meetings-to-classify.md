## How to mark these

Put one code per row. Definitions matter more than speed — the register split is what keeps the largest paid outcome out of the conversion rate.

| code | meaning |
|---|---|
| `INT` | **Interview.** A hiring conversation for a specific role, formal or informal |
| `OPP` | **Opportunity.** A real conversation about working together that was not an interview for a posted role — founder chats, advisory, contract feelers |
| `VEND` | **Vendor or tool.** They were selling to you, or you were evaluating their product |
| `COMM` | **Community or mentorship.** Peer, mentor, or network conversation with no specific opportunity |
| `WORK` | **Engagement work.** A meeting that belongs to ENG-A through ENG-E, not to the job search |
| `PERS` | **Personal.** Not job-search related |
| `?` | You do not remember. **This is a valid answer and better than a guess** |

Two things worth knowing before you start:

- **`booked_by = THEM`** means the counterparty booked your scheduling page, so it is counterparty-initiated by artifact. Those are `OPP` unless you applied somewhere first.
- **`booked_by = YOU`** means you booked theirs.
- Anything you mark `INT` enters the interview count and changes a headline number. Anything you mark `?` stays out of the count and is reported as unclassified. **Both are defensible. A guess is not.**
